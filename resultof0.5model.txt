================================================================================
3. CAVEATS
================================================================================
- Scenarios are simulated with deterministic fault injection; only the model's
  planning is real.
- 0.5B is a wiring/quality-floor check, not a benchmark of model capability.
  A larger model (e.g. qwen3.5:4b as documented in the README) is required for
  meaningful success-rate comparisons.
- Single-run, 3-scenario differences are noise. Use repetitions for any claim.
- The llama-server process was stopped after these runs; reproduce with the
  command in section 1.
